⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

  • A - Snap LED/Button to pins
  • W - Create wire (hover pin first)
  • R - Rotate LED (hold & drag)
  • Delete - Remove component/wire

🖱️ Mouse Controls

  • Button: Left-click = Press | Right-click + Drag = Move
  • Other Components: Left-click + Drag = Move
  • Wires: Click pin → Drag → Release on pin
